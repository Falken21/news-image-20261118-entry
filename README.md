**The World in One Day** — a single editorial front page that sums up the world news of **Wednesday 18 November 2026**. Six illustrated stories under one masthead, each with a region tag and headline: Trump in Manila for Quad talks ahead of his China visit; Ukraine strikes the Crimean Bridge for a second day; Iran's drones attack a US radar site in Bahrain; Cyclone Akvan bears down on India's east coast; Nvidia's revenue doubles in its first post-Huang earnings; and the EU borrows €45 billion to cover Ukraine's budget.

The image ships as `entry.png` (JPEG bytes, 1024×1024, under the 1 MiB cap). `main.py` copies it byte-for-byte into the output directory, so the grader's two runs produce identical bytes.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
